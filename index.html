About

A designer who ships finished work.

I'm Gurpreet — a graphic and UI/UX designer working with founders, studios and small brands to turn rough ideas into shipped identities and products. My work spans e‑commerce storefronts, SaaS interfaces, brand systems and the everyday visual assets — banners, posters, social content — that keep a brand consistent. I care about clarity first: every layout should tell a visitor exactly what to do next.
